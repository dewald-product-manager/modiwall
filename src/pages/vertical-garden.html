What a living wall needs

If your space is missing one of these, tell us. There is usually a way, and when there is not we will say so.

Light

Daylight, or grow lights where there is too little. Indirect light is enough for many forest-floor species, as at Netcare Pinehaven.

Water

A reservoir and pump at the base of the wall. The pump runs on a digital timer and the water is recirculated.

Power

A plug point for the pump, and for lights if the wall needs them.

A hand

Yours or ours. Plants are pruned, checked and replaced on a weekly or monthly visit.
