Mosquitoes

They need standing water, open to the air, for the week or so it takes to raise a family. A saucer under a pot is ideal. The inside of a Modiwall is not: the water is enclosed in the panels, it moves every day, and the reservoir is covered.

Gnats

The small dark flies around houseplants are fungus gnats, and they are drawn to soil that never dries. Because each vessel drinks through a wick, the surface stays drier than a watered pot. If they do appear it is a sign that something is too wet, and the cure is to find out what.

Aphids, scale and mealybug

These arrive on new plants, on clothes, through windows. They are the reason the first thing done on every visit is to look. Found early, they are wiped away. Found late, a vessel is lifted out and its neighbours stay where they are.

Outside

An outdoor wall will bring life. Sunbirds to the bulbine, bees to the plectranthus, a gecko who pays rent. Most people find that this was what they wanted without having known to ask. If you would rather not have bees beside a child’s pool, we leave out what flowers.

And snakes

A snake wants cover at ground level and something to eat. A wall that begins above a clean plinth, and is kept trimmed, has little to offer it. Ivy over a heap of stones is another matter.
