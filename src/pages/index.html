Which wall in your house is still bare?See it on your wallMore private walls

There is only one.

  1. The panel

    Patented, and the reason it works. Panels lock into one another and carry water through channels you will never see.

  2. The grid

    Fixed over the panel, it holds every plant in its own place.

  3. The vessel

    Each plant has its own. If one tires, we lift it out and its neighbours never know.

  4. The water

    The wall waters itself, then gathers the water back to use again.

  5. The wall

    As wide and as high as the room deserves. Flat, curved or on a slant.

One house, one guarantee

  1. Designed by us.

    Drawn for one address, around its light, its water and the way you use the room.

  2. Made by us.

    A patented system in recycled polymer, moulded in Cape Town. You cannot buy it from anyone else.

  3. Planted by us.

    Species chosen for your light first and your interior second, set by hand.

  4. Guaranteed by us.

    No subcontractors to chase and no parts to match. The people who answer for it are the people who made it.

Why other living walls fail

The water stops

Ours circulates. Every panel feeds the one below it, and nothing depends on someone remembering.

The plants were wrong

We read the light before we choose a leaf. At Netcare Pinehaven that meant forest species, happy in an atrium.

One gap spreads

Every plant stands alone in its vessel, so one can be replaced without disturbing the rest.

The installer moves on

We made it, so we stay. Our team returns weekly or monthly.
