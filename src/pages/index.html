Come home to it.

Dusk, one switch, and the wall is the room.

There is only one.

  1. The panel

    Patented, and the reason it works. Panels lock into one another and carry water through channels you will never see.

  2. The grid

    Fixed over the panel, it holds every plant in its own place.

  3. The vessel

    Each plant has its own. If one tires, we lift it out and its neighbours never know.

  4. The water

    The wall waters itself, then gathers the water back to use again.

  5. The wall

    As wide and as high as the room deserves, over one steel reservoir that holds the pump and its timer. Flat, curved or on a slant.

One house, one guarantee

  1. Designed by us.

    Drawn for one address, around its light, its water and the way you use the room.

    Light
    Daylight first. Grow lights only where there is too little.
    Water
    A hidden reservoir, a pump and a timer, planned into the room.
    Surface
    Flat, curved or on a slant, indoors or out.
    {{img skylight | (min-width:900px) 52vw, 100vw}}

  2. Made by us.

    It began with two friends, a garage and a 3D printer. The system has held a South African patent since 2016, and is moulded in Cape Town.

    Panel
    500 × 500 mm. Edges slide together, male into female.
    Vessels
    Twenty to a panel, each lifting out by hand.
    Material
    Recycled polypropylene, injection moulded.
    {{img piece-panel | (min-width:900px) 96vw, 160vw}}

  3. Planted by us.

    Species chosen for your light first and your interior second, set by hand.

    Density
    About eighty plants to the square metre.
    Roots
    Every plant alone in its own vessel.
    Choice
    Forest species for shade, sun lovers for the terrace.
    {{img zanzibar-detail | (min-width:900px) 52vw, 100vw}}

  4. Guaranteed by us.

    No subcontractors to chase and no parts to match. The people who answer for it are the people who made it.

    Visits
    Weekly or monthly, by our own team.
    Plants
    One vessel is changed. Its neighbours stay rooted.
    Terms
    Set out in writing with your quotation.

Why other living walls fail

The water stops

Ours circulates. Every panel feeds the one below it, and nothing depends on someone remembering.

The plants were wrong

We read the light before we choose a leaf. At Netcare Pinehaven that meant forest species, happy in an atrium.

One gap spreads

Every plant stands alone in its vessel, so one can be replaced without disturbing the rest.

The installer moves on

We made it, so we stay. Our team returns weekly or monthly.
