Everybody imagines it beside the stove. It nearly always belongs outside the kitchen door.

Sun first

Herbs are plants of dry hillsides. They want four to six hours of real sun. A bright kitchen has perhaps a tenth of that. Indoors they grow long, pale and polite.

What does well

Thyme and oregano, which fall. Rosemary at the top, where it is driest. Mint lower down, in a vessel of its own, where for once it cannot run away. Strawberries, because they hang as though they had been designed for it.

What does not

Basil outdoors in a Highveld winter. Coriander in a hot summer. Anything you would not walk five steps to pick.

It has been studied

Researchers at the University of Pretoria grew African vegetables on living walls for six months. It can be done. It is farming, though, and farms are bare between crops.

Our advice

Give one corner to herbs, at hand height, beside the braai. Let the rest be something that looks well all year. These are the mixes we would put around it.
