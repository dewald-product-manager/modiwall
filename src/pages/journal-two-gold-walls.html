South Africa’s landscapers give out their own prizes, and they are hard to impress. Twice, a wall built with Modiwall has come home with gold.

Netcare Pinehaven, 2016

A hospital atrium is a room where people wait, often for news. The architect was Pieter Oosthuizen of VDO Consulting. The difficulty was light. The planting relies on what comes in indirectly, so it was drawn from plants that are at home in shade.

Bidvest TopTurf built it. The South African Landscapers Institute gave it gold for specialised landscape construction.

Sun City, 2017

A year later the brief was a tree. Steel, standing under a skylight in the Entertainment Centre, and green from trunk to branch. Nothing about it was flat. Panels that swivel to follow a curve earned their keep.

Bidvest TopTurf built this one too. It took gold for interior plantscaping, and African Design Magazine wrote about it.

What they share

Neither began with a product. Each began with one room, its light, and the people who would stand in it. That is still how every wall begins, whether it hangs in a resort or beside a bath.
