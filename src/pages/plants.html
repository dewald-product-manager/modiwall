Nine provinces, nine plant lists

Weather decides what will live outdoors. Choose yours.

Gauteng

Summer thunderstorms, dry sunny winters, and frost.

Plants that rest in winter and stand up again in spring. Tender ones go on a north-facing or covered wall.

  • Blue fescue
  • Stonecrop
  • Bulbine
  • Lily turf
  • Silver helichrysum

Western Cape

Rain in winter, long dry summers, wind for most of the year. The coast is nearly frost free.

Silver leaves and succulents that can wait for water, and that do not mind a south-easter.

  • Silver helichrysum
  • Pig’s ear
  • Campfire crassula
  • Spekboom
  • Bulbine

KwaZulu-Natal

Warm, humid and green, with no frost on the coast. Salt and wind near the sea.

Soft foliage and shade lovers. Inland and in the Midlands, where there is frost, the list changes.

  • Spurflower
  • Asparagus fern
  • Hen and chickens
  • Lily turf
  • Campfire crassula

Eastern Cape

Frost-free coast, cold interior, and dry valleys between them.

This is spekboom country. Succulents inland, spurflower by the sea.

  • Spekboom
  • Campfire crassula
  • Pig’s ear
  • Spurflower
  • Bulbine

Free State

Hot summers, hard frost, wide skies.

Grasses and stonecrop that shrug off a cold night. Shelter for everything else.

  • Blue fescue
  • Stonecrop
  • Lily turf
  • Bulbine

North West

Summer rain and not much of it. Hot days, cold winter nights.

Plants that keep water in their leaves.

  • Bulbine
  • Stonecrop
  • Pig’s ear
  • Spekboom

Limpopo

Bushveld heat, summer rain, only light frost.

Sun and drought first. Shade planting under a roof does very well.

  • Spekboom
  • Bulbine
  • Campfire crassula
  • Asparagus fern
  • Hen and chickens

Mpumalanga

Two climates. The Highveld freezes. The Lowveld is nearly tropical.

On the Highveld, plant as for Gauteng. In the Lowveld, as for the KwaZulu-Natal coast.

  • Blue fescue
  • Stonecrop
  • Spurflower
  • Asparagus fern
  • Hen and chickens

Northern Cape

The driest province. Fierce sun, and nights that fall below freezing.

Succulents, grasses and nothing that sulks.

  • Pig’s ear
  • Campfire crassula
  • Stonecrop
  • Bulbine
  • Spekboom
