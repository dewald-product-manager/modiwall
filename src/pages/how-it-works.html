There is only one.

  1. The panel

    Patented, and the reason it works. Panels lock into one another and carry water through channels you will never see.

  2. The grid

    Fixed over the panel, it holds every plant in its own place.

  3. The vessel

    Each plant has its own. If one tires, we lift it out and its neighbours never know.

  4. The water

    The wall waters itself, then gathers the water back to use again.

  5. The wall

    As wide and as high as the room deserves. Flat, curved or on a slant.

For the specification

Drawings and data are available to architects on request.

Module

500 × 500 mm panel. Four panels to the square metre, extended in any direction.

Planting

20 clip-in pots per panel, about 80 plants per square metre.

Material

Recycled polypropylene, injection moulded in Cape Town.

Irrigation

Pump, ring main and reservoir on a digital timer, engineered with the wall and guaranteed with it. Panels feed one another from the top.

Surfaces

Flat, curved and slanted walls, indoors and outdoors. Parts swivel, so panels follow a curve.

Installation

Complete, by our team. The system is not sold in parts.
