Everyone lowers their voice to ask this, as if the house might hear. The answer is reassuring.

The figure

Fully saturated, planted and grown, a Modiwall weighs about 37 kilograms for every square metre. Some 14 of that is the system, 18 is wet soil and 5 is plants. It is the wet figure that matters. Any supplier who quotes you a dry one has answered a different question.

What that compares with

Thirty millimetres of marble or granite is roughly 80 kilograms per square metre before the adhesive. A large mirror is more than 25. So in most houses the garden is the lighter of the options on the mood board.

It is the wet figure that matters.

Brick and concrete

A sound masonry wall takes it easily, because the load is shared by many fixings rather than hung from a few. We check the wall first: what it is made of, what is inside it, and whether it is as straight as it looks.

Partitions and steel

Drywall needs fixings into the studs, or a light frame that takes the weight to the floor. Columns, curves and free-standing screens are engineered one by one. At Sun City the system was carried around a steel tree under a skylight, which is as far from a flat wall as we have been asked to go.

When to ask an engineer

When the wall is taller than a storey. When it hangs over people. When it stands outside in the wind. And whenever anything above it is precious. We give your engineer the loads and the fixings in writing, early, so that nobody has to guess.
