Before the keys are down you have already decided how the day went. That is a great deal to ask of a console table and a mirror.

Outside the door

A porch or a drive court is bright shade, which nearly everything enjoys. Frame the planting in the stone of the house and light it from above. Guests will stand in front of it while they wait, so put the bell there.

Inside it

The best wall is the one facing you as the door opens, or the one at the turn of the passage that leads you on. Keep it off the path of the door itself. Allow the width of two people passing with bags.

Put the bell where they will have to look at it.

What to plant

One calm mix. This is not the place for twenty species. Canopy if it is bright, Velvet if it is not.

A companion

A bronze, a bowl of water, a bench. Planting makes an excellent background, and an entrance likes to have one thing in front of it.

At night

Put it on the circuit that comes on when you turn into the drive. You will be home a minute before you arrive.

Photograph your entrance and see it planted.
