A living wall moves, flowers, scents the air and is never the same twice. An artificial one asks for nothing. Both belong in good houses. These four questions usually settle it.

Is there daylight?

If you can read comfortably by the window light at that spot for most of the day, a plant can probably live there. If not, the choice is grow lights or an artificial wall.

Is there water and a plug?

A living Modiwall circulates its own water from a hidden reservoir, and needs one plug point for the pump. Where neither can be brought to the wall, choose artificial.

Can someone reach it?

A living wall is a garden, and a gardener has to get to it. Above a bed, behind a bar or six metres over a stair with no landing, think hard.

How close will people stand?

From across a room, a well composed artificial wall is convincing. At arm’s length nothing replaces a leaf that is really there.
