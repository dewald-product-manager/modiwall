Nobody remembers a ceiling. Guests remember where they sat.

The seat

Put the planting where people will face it for an hour. Behind a banquette it becomes the view from every other table. At the end of a long room it gives the eye somewhere to go.

The courtyard

Four white walls and an umbrella is a car park with cutlery. Plant one of them and the temperature drops, in every sense.

The spa

Warm, humid and dim, which is the forest floor exactly. A wall at the end of a pool doubles itself in the water. Light it from above and keep everything else dark.

What can go wrong

Grease, heat and people with trays. We keep it out of all three. We also keep it out of the way of the cleaning team, who are quick and have hoses.

The programme

Most of our hospitality walls are tended before service. A plant that has had enough of Friday night is lifted out on Monday and another takes its place. At Zanzibar the wall is two storeys of fern and philodendron. Walk through it.
