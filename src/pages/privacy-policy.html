Privacy

We collect only what you type into our quote form or send us, and we use it only to reply to you.

What we collect

Your name, email address and, if you give them, your phone number, town and details of your wall. Giving us these is voluntary. Without a name and email address we cannot send you a quote.

Why we collect it

To answer your request, send a quotation and, if you become a client, do the work. We do not sell your details or share them with third parties for marketing.

Where it goes

The form sends an email to Modiwall through our hosting provider. To stop spam, the server keeps a scrambled code derived from your internet address, with the times of recent requests.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. Links to social media and Google Maps open those sites only when you click them.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and object to its use. Email info@modiwall.com or call +27 76 874 0299.

You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Who is responsible

Modiwall Vertical Gardens, Pebble Springs, Highveld, Centurion, South Africa.
