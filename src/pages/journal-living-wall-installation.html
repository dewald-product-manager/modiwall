The longest part happens somewhere else. Your plants are grown on in their own vessels until they are the size you expected them to be.

Before we come

We will have stood in front of the wall, measured it, and looked at the light at its worst hour. You will have a drawing, a planting and a price. Your electrician will have left one plug point where we asked.

The wall is prepared

Floors are covered. The surface is checked and waterproofed, past the edges and down to the floor. Then comes the only noise of the week, which is drilling.

The panels

They slide into one another, each foot seated in the head of the one below. The grid goes on in front. The reservoir is set at the foot, the pump in the reservoir, the feed along the top. Then we run water for long enough to be bored by it, and look for anything that should not be wet.

The planting

Vessel by vessel, from the bottom up. It is quiet, and most people stay to watch. By the end of the day it is a garden, a little thin. Give it three months.

And after

The timer is set, the light is set, and you get the list of everything growing in your house. We come back in the first week, because that is when plants decide whether they like you.
