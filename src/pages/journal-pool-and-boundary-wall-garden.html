We build a great many walls in this country, mostly to keep something out. The one at the end of the pool is usually the largest blank surface on the property, and the one you look at most.

A garden with no bed

Paving often runs right up to a pool wall, with no soil to plant in. A vertical garden needs none. It hangs on the wall, waters itself from a reservoir at the foot and gives the terrace back its edge.

What the sun asks

An outdoor wall lives harder than an indoor one. It takes the full afternoon, the wind, and heat thrown up from the paving. The planting has to be chosen for all three. Species that glow in a bathroom would scorch here by lunch.

It is the largest blank surface on the property, and the one you look at most.

What you gain

Privacy without the fortress. A softer sound: a 2020 study measured significantly less noise at green walls than at bare ones in a city centre. And a view that changes with the season, which plaster never has.

Before you plant

Have the wall itself checked, because a planted and watered garden is heavy. Know which way it faces. Find out where the nearest power is. Then try the idea on a photograph, or send us the wall.
