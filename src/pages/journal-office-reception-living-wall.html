A reception says one thing while you are waiting for the person who will say the rest. Marble says money. A screen says television. A wall of leaves says that somebody looked after something.

What it is for

Not oxygen. Plants do not clean the air of a building, whatever the brochure says. They do something more useful. In field experiments in working offices, people concentrated better and were happier when plants were there. The research is here.

What it does to the room

It quietens it. Receptions are hard, tall and full of phones, and foliage breaks up the echo.

It says that somebody looked after something.

The logo

Do not write it in plants. They grow. Set it in front, in brass or steel, and let the planting be the ground it stands on.
