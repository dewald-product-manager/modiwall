When we advise it

We make living walls. We advise an artificial one only where a plant would suffer.

No daylight

Windowless rooms, where grow lights would spoil the interior.

No water

Places where a reservoir and pump cannot be hidden.

No reach

Ceilings and heights no gardener should have to climb to.
