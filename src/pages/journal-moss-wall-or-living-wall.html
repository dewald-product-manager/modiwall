Moss walls are handsome. They are also, nearly always, preserved, which is a gentle way of saying that they have finished growing.

What preserved moss is good at

It asks for nothing. No light, no water, no reservoir, no visits. It is thin and light, and it takes the edge off the noise of a hard room. For a logo behind a desk or a panel in a lift lobby, it is a sensible material.

What it cannot do

Change. It is the same on Tuesday as it was on Monday, until slowly it is a little paler and a little drier. It does not like sun, or a room that is dry one season and damp the next. It is not for outdoors in our climate.

What a living wall asks

Light, water and somebody who comes. In return it has new leaves in spring. It moves when a door opens. Children touch it. A guest will lean in to see whether it is real, and it is.

How to choose

  • No daylight and no room for a lamp: moss, or an artificial wall.
  • A flat graphic, a logo, a small panel: moss.
  • A wall you will sit beside, indoors or out: living.
