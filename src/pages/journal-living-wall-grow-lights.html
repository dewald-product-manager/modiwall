There are two lights on a good indoor wall. One is for the plants, and it is work. The other is for you.

First, measure

Your phone will do. Hold it against the wall, facing the room, at noon and again at four. Our article on how much light an indoor wall needs gives the numbers to compare it with.

If it is not enough

Add lamps made for plants, in a neutral white. They are set close, in a line along the ceiling, and aimed down the face so that the foot of the wall gets its share. A timer runs them for the middle of the day, when the room is bright anyway and nobody notices.

Not purple

Pink and violet lamps belong in a greenhouse. They make leaves look black and people look ill. White light grows plants perfectly well.

Pink lamps belong in a greenhouse.

Then the evening

For after dark, a warmer and much lower light, raking down from above. Every leaf throws a shadow on the next. You can try it, lamp by lamp, in the evening room on our home page.

Plan it with the ceiling

  • A recess or a pelmet deep enough to hide the lamp.
  • Two circuits: one for the plants, one for you.
  • Room to reach it.
