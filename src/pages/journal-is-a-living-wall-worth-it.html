What you are paying for

A structure, a water system, several hundred plants and a year of growing them before you saw them. Then a person who comes. The last is the part people forget, and the part that decides whether the rest was money well spent.

What comes back

Something that is different in the morning. A quieter room. A house that guests describe to other people. If you work in front of it, probably a better afternoon. None of it fits in a column.

None of it fits in a column.

What does not

Do not buy one for the air. Do not buy one as an investment that will sell the house. It may help. It is not why you do it.

Who should not

  • If the only wall is dark and you will not light it.
  • If the house stands empty for half the year and nobody will visit it.
  • If you would like it to look the same for ever.

For the first, consider an artificial wall. For the last, stone.

A test

Stand where it would be. Count how many times you pass it in a day. If the answer is more than five, see it planted, and then decide.
