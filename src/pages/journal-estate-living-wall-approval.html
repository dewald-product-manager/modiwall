Is it seen?

If it faces the road, a neighbour or common property, assume that you must ask. Inside your own courtyard you often need not. The guidelines will say which, and they are worth an evening.

Whose wall is it?

A boundary is frequently shared. Find out before you fix anything to it. If it is shared, a letter now is easier than a letter later. A wall can also stand free on its own frame, a hand’s breadth inside the line.

What to send

  • One picture of your wall with the planting on it. Make it here and save it.
  • The size, and the weight when wet.
  • How water is kept in, in two sentences. These will do.
  • The plants, with none that are listed as invasive in your province.
  • Who will look after it, and how often.

In sectional title

The outside of the building belongs to everyone. Weight and waterproofing go to the body corporate’s engineer. A wall that keeps its own water in a reservoir, and drips on nobody, is an easier conversation.
