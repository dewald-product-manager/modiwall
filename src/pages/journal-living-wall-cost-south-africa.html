Ask for the price of a living wall and you will usually be given a figure for a square metre. It is a poor guide. Five things decide what a wall costs, and area is only the first.

Area and shape

A square metre of Modiwall carries about eighty plants, each in its own vessel. A larger wall shares one reservoir, pump and timer across more planting. A very small wall carries all of that alone.

Light

A bright courtyard needs nothing from us. A dark stairwell may need grow lights, and the electrician who goes with them. Measure the light before you settle on the wall.

Reach

A wall you can touch is simple. One that climbs a double volume needs scaffolding or a lift on the day it is planted, and on the days it is tended.

The planting

Young plants usually cost less and fill in over time. Mature plants look finished sooner.

Care

This is the figure that is easiest to leave out of a quote, and the one that decides whether you still like your wall in three years. Ours is tended weekly or monthly by the people who made it.

The cheapest wall on paper is often the one with nobody coming back.

Four questions for any quote

  • Does it include the water system, and who answers for it?
  • What happens, and what does it cost, when a plant dies?
  • Who tends the wall, how often, and for how much?
  • Is the guarantee in writing?
