Green wall installation at hospital

Netcare Pinehaven, Gauteng

The magazine reported the gold award Bidvest TopTurf won from the South African Landscapers Institute in 2016, in specialised landscape construction, for the vertical garden at Netcare Pinehaven on Johannesburg’s West Rand.

Planting on the green wall at the hospital comprises species that are easily acclimatised to an indoor environment as they grow naturally in shady, forest areas.

In the atrium the plants rely on indirect daylight alone. The piece sets the wall within Netcare’s wider idea of a healing building: larger windows, gardens between the wards, and planted atria that draw warm air up and out, so there is less to cool.

Jonathan Ferguson of Bidvest TopTurf, who worked with architect Pieter Oosthuizen of VDO Consulting, introduced the readers to “the new South African product for vertical gardens, Modiwall”, every component of it made in a Cape Town factory.

Ornamental green ‘tree’ structure in Sun City’s Entertainment Centre

Green walls

Dawid Fraser, operations manager of Bidvest TopTurf North West, described a slanted vertical garden in the shape of a great tree, built during a major revamp of the Entertainment Centre around a central steel structure beneath an extensive skylight.

The ample indoor light was seen as an opportunity to use live plants rather than artificial.

TopTurf proposed covering the steel with the Modiwall planted wall system, “to enhance the grandeur and the environmental value of the ‘tree sculpture’”. The interior plantscaping of the Centre took gold at the 2017 South African Landscapers Institute Awards of Excellence.

Living walls: upscaling their performance as green infrastructure

The Vicinity and Modiwall are locally manufactured systems comprising recyclable plastic pots for plants and growing mediums.

The study compares living wall systems available in South Africa before growing African vegetables on two others in Pretoria. Modiwall is among the locally made systems it describes.
