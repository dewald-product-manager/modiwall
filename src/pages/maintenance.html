Kept by its makers. This is one visit.

First, we look.

Every plant is inspected before anything is touched.

What has finished comes away.

We remove, prune and shape, so the light reaches the leaves behind.

One vessel out. One in.

Where a plant has tired, we replant its vessel. Its neighbours stay rooted.

Then the water.

We test the soil and the irrigation, so a fault is found before the plants find it.

Then what holds it.

We look over the panels and the fixings, and tell you what needs attention.

And we leave it as it should be.

Until the next time, in a week or a month.
