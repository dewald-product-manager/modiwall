What a visit covers

Plants

We inspect them, remove dead or damaged foliage, prune and shape, treat pests and disease, pull weeds and replant pots where needed.

Water

We check soil moisture and the irrigation system, so a fault is found before the plants find it.

Structure

We look over the panels and fixings and tell you about anything that needs attention.

Cleaning

We clean the wall and the area around it before we leave.
