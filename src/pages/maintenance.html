Kept by its makers.

A living wall is a garden, and a garden needs a gardener. This is one visit. First, we look: every plant is inspected before anything is touched.

What has finished comes away.

Dead or damaged foliage is removed. The rest is pruned and shaped, weeds are pulled, and pests and disease are treated.

One vessel out. One in.

Where a plant has tired, we replant its pot. Its neighbours stay rooted, and the new one closes the gap.

Then the water.

We check soil moisture and the irrigation system, so a fault is found before the plants find it.

Then what holds it.

We look over the panels and the fixings, and tell you about anything that needs attention.

And we leave it as it should be.

The wall and the area around it are cleaned before we go. Until the next time, in a week or a month.
