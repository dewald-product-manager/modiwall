A living wall that arrives late is hung on a building. One that arrives early is part of it. The difference is five decisions, made while the drawings are open.

One. The module

The panel is 500 by 500 millimetres. Set out openings, returns and ends in half metres and nothing needs cutting. Four panels make a square metre and carry about eighty plants.

Two. What it hangs on

A planted, watered wall has weight. Your engineer should have it from the start. Waterproof the substrate behind.

Three. Water and power

Allow for a reservoir at the foot and one plug point for the pump and timer. The water circulates, so there is no constant feed to waste.

Four. Light

Show the glazing, the orientation and any overhang. Where daylight falls short, design the grow lights in, so that they are seen as lighting. Here are the levels to aim for.

Five. Somebody has to reach it

Every wall is tended. Give the gardener a floor to stand on, or an anchor above.
