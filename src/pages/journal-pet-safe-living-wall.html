A wall of leaves at nose height is an invitation. Most animals look once and lose interest. Some do not, and it is better to have planned for the ones that do not.

Safe to be within reach

Spider plant, Boston fern and the round-leaved peperomia are all listed by the ASPCA as non-toxic to cats and dogs. Between them you have an arching grass, a soft frond and a glossy leaf, which is enough for a composition.

Not to be chewed

Philodendron, pothos, peace lily, umbrella plant and dracaena are listed as toxic to both. For the most part they irritate the mouth and stomach. They are also some of the best wall plants there are, so the question becomes where they stand, not whether.

The question is where they stand, not whether.

Plan by height

Begin the planting above a stone or timber plinth. Keep the first metre for safe species. Let the philodendrons start above the back of the sofa. For a cat that climbs, choose only from the safe list, and say so when you commission the wall.

Water and soil

In a Modiwall the water is inside the panels and the soil is in small vessels, so there is no bowl to drink from and nothing to dig. Feed is given in measured amounts by our team.

If something is eaten

Keep the list of what is planted. We leave one with you. Call your vet, and tell them the name.
