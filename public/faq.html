Questions

What does a Modiwall cost?

Every wall is priced for its own place: the area, the plants, the light and how we reach it. Send us the wall and we will reply with a figure.

Can it live indoors?

Yes. Many of our walls are inside: in bathrooms, stairwells, receptions and hospital atriums. We choose species that grow naturally in shade, and we tell you plainly if a room is too dark.

How is it watered?

The wall waters itself. A pump on a digital timer lifts water from a hidden reservoir to the top. It runs down through channels inside the panels and returns.

What if a plant dies?

We lift out its vessel and place a new one. The plants around it stay rooted.

Who looks after it?

We do. Our own team visits weekly or monthly to prune, replant, treat pests and check the irrigation. See what a visit covers.

Is it guaranteed?

Yes, by the people who designed and made it. The terms are set out in writing with your quotation.

Can it follow a curve or a slant?

Yes. Parts swivel so panels follow a curve, and at Sun City the system clads a slanted steel tree.

How much space does it need?

It needs a reservoir at its base, a plug point for the pump, and daylight or discreet grow lights.

Should I choose living or artificial?

Living, wherever a plant can be happy. Where there is no daylight, water or access, we will tell you and offer an artificial wall instead.

What is it made of?

Recycled polypropylene, injection moulded in Cape Town. The system is patented.

Can I buy the panels on their own?

No. A Modiwall is designed, planted, installed and guaranteed as one wall.

Do you work outside Gauteng?

Yes. We are based in Centurion, work across South Africa, and take commissions in other countries.

What is your B-BBEE status?

Modiwall is a Level 4 exempt micro enterprise (EME).
