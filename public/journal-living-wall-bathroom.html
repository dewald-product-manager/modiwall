The bathroom is a better garden than you think

Warmth, steam and soft light. Most houseplants would choose it themselves.

By Modiwall. 24 November 2026. 1 minute read.

Think about where our favourite indoor plants come from. Damp, warm, shaded places under taller trees. Then think about a bathroom on a winter morning.

Why it works

Ferns and philodendrons resent dry air more than almost anything else indoors. A bathroom is the one room in the house that gives them the humidity they came from. Frosted glass, so often the bathroom window, turns hard sun into exactly the soft light they like.

Where to put it

Beside the bath, where you will look at it for longest. Behind a basin, if the mirror can float in front. At the end of a wet room, where it becomes the view. Keep it out of the direct line of the shower, because nobody enjoys being hosed with hot water.

A bath with a garden beside it is a different kind of bath.

Plan it before the tiler

  • A place for the reservoir at the foot of the wall.
  • One plug point for the pump, where regulations allow it.
  • Waterproofing carried behind the panels.
  • Room for somebody to stand and tend it.

The marble bathroom in our walk was drawn with the room. The stone, the light and the wall were one decision. If yours is still on paper, this is the moment to send it.
